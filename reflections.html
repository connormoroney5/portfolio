General education reflection

One of the requirements for CPRE 4940 is to write a reflection about the general education courses you decided to take. Through writing this reflection, I was able to understand the value of general education courses. I realized that many of the problems engineers face cannot be solved with pure technical skills. The general education courses helped fill the gaps in my knowledge and become a more well-rounded student and engineer.

Ethics paper

One of the required courses for Computer Engineers is CPRE 2320: Professional and Ethical Issues in Computer Engineering. As engineers, our main goal is to solve problems to make the world a better place. This class helps us ensure that our contributions are truly impacting others in a positive way. Through this final reflection, I was able to talk about an ethical delima I came up with that actually could appear for someone like me, who specializes in frontend development. I wanted to prepare myself for a situation I could actually face and find out how ethics are still important for UI/UX designers.
