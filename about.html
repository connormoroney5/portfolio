About me

Where I am headed and how I got here.

Background

I grew up in Dubuque, Iowa. In Elementry School, I was invited to join the school's FIRST Lego League (FLL) team. I really enjoyed the experience designing, coding, and competing with robots. I continued with FLL through middle school and eventually joined the FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) team in high school. FTC took things to a whole new level. I spent countless hours with my team before and after school striving to build the best robot possible. We even won an award at State for the first time in school history. Through FTC, I knew that I wanted to become an Engineer. I loved designing solutions and working as a team. Now, I get to continue this passion as a career. Although it took a lot of work, I can finally consider myself an engineer.

Career objective

After graduation, I hope to continue full time with Thingz Inc. as a Full-stack Developer. Although I will be done with school, I plan on keeping an open mind and trying to learn new things. I am not to sure where I will be 10 years from now, but I know that I will use the problem solving techniques I have learned to conquer any endeavors I face and make a strong impact on the world.

In my free time, I hope to give back to the program that helped me become who I am today. I recently found out that my high school dropped their FTC program because they were unable to find a coach. As an FTC Alumn, I know the importance of having STEM-based clubs for students to participate in. That is why I want to bring back the FTC program to my highschool and help coach it. I want to make a difference in people's lives and show them that Engineering can be fun!
